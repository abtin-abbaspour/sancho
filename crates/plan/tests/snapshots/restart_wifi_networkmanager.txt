[verified recipe · network.restart_wifi v1] Restart Wi-Fi (NetworkManager: radio off and on; iwd: restart the daemon)
Risk: medium — you will be asked to confirm

  1. Turn the Wi-Fi radio off
     $ nmcli radio wifi off
     can be undone

  2. Turn the Wi-Fi radio back on
     $ nmcli radio wifi on
     can be undone

Undo: every step can be undone with `sancho undo`
